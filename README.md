## 🧠 About Me

I'm an AI engineering student and freelance developer specializing in **Next.js + LLM integration** — building full-stack, AI-powered web products for the Arab market and beyond. I combine **Applied AI** (Computer Vision, Deep Learning, NLP) with **modern frontend architecture** to ship real systems, not demos.

- 🚀 **Currently Building:** [Rowad](#-rowad--under-development) — a zero-commission freelance platform for the Arab tech community.
- 🌐 **Community:** Founder of **Opportunity Hub** — a WhatsApp community where computing students share courses and opportunities.
- 🎯 **Current Focus:** AI-powered SaaS products, LLM-integrated tooling, and multi-modal AI systems (Vision + Language).
- 💼 **Freelancing:** Available for AI/SaaS + frontend work — Mostaql, Khamsat, Upwork.
- 💡 **Motto:** Build real systems. Solve real problems. Leave something behind.
