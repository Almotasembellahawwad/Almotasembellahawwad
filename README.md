## Selected Work

### [Audiomap](https://github.com/Almotasembellahawwad/Audiomap)

Voice and text become interactive mind maps in under a second. Arabic and English transcription, LLM-powered structure, a draggable React Flow canvas, local-first storage, and multi-format export.

`Next.js 16` `TypeScript` `Whisper v3` `LLaMA 3.3 70B` `Groq LPU` `React Flow`

[Live product ↗](https://audiomap-five.vercel.app/)

### [NewsPulse](https://github.com/Almotasembellahawwad/newspulse-ai-dashboard)

A real-time news and market intelligence dashboard that aggregates global RSS feeds, analyzes sentiment with LLaMA 3.3, extracts market entities, maps concepts, and reads daily briefs aloud.

`Next.js 15` `TypeScript` `Groq` `LLaMA 3.3 70B` `CoinGecko` `React Flow`

[Live product ↗](https://newspulse-ai-dashboard.vercel.app/)

### [Verve](https://github.com/Almotasembellahawwad/Verve)

An open-source design-taste layer between language models and generated interfaces—built to detect generic visual decisions and guide output toward deliberate design systems.

`Next.js` `TypeScript` `Design Systems` `AI Tooling`

[Live experiment ↗](https://verve-chi.vercel.app/)

### [ARIA Voice Companion](https://github.com/Almotasembellahawwad/Aria-Voice-Companion)

An emotionally aware Arabic–English voice companion with sub-second streaming responses, adaptive voice behavior, conversational emotion tracking, and an independent Telegram client.

`Python` `FastAPI` `WebSockets` `Groq` `LLaMA 3` `Orpheus TTS`

### [Windows XP Interactive Portfolio](https://github.com/Almotasembellahawwad/windows-xp-portfolio)

A browser-based operating-system experience with draggable windows, a functional CMD and Notepad, local persistence, mini-games, system audio, and full boot and shutdown states.

`Next.js 14` `Zustand` `Framer Motion` `Three.js`

[Boot the system ↗](https://windows-xp-portfolio-ivory.vercel.app/)
